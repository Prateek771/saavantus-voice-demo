# Saavantus design system

Authority: user-approved design/01-homepage.png, 02-real-estate.png, 03-healthcare.png and WEBSITE_DESIGN_AND_COPY.md. Implement this visual world rather than proposing another concept.

Ivory canvas #f7f4ee; ink #17242a; accessible action red #b93626; decorative vermilion #da4c38; warm rules #dad9d2. DM Sans body and headings, restrained italic DM Serif Display phrases, Noto Sans Devanagari script fallback. Fonts are self-hosted through Fontsource.

Use an editorial homepage and a two-panel industry walkthrough: light conversation, dark structured result. Stack panels on mobile and keep simulation status visible. Neutral appointment statuses; no green success treatment implying a real booking. Architecture is illustrative. Thin-stroke SVG icons and authored geometric waveform linework; no external embeds.

Marketing pages persuade; walkthrough panels support an operational task. Every sample record and script must remain clearly distinguished from a working voice or CRM integration. Keyboard focus, native form controls, readable contrast and reduced motion are part of the design.
